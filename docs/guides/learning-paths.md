Choose the path closest to your goal. The time estimates are planning aids; take longer whenever an experiment needs explanation. Progress stays in this browser and can be exported.

## Programming by robot type

Follow a robot's role and mechanism arrangement through its software. The [concept-to-programming matrix](../robot-types/README.md) maps all fifteen unbuilt research concepts to these seven families.

1. [Chassis-aimed shooter](../robot-types/fixed-shooter.md)
2. [Turreted shooter](../robot-types/turret-shooter.md)
3. [Intake, conveyor and hopper](../robot-types/piece-handling.md)
4. [Elevator and wrist](../robot-types/elevator-wrist.md)
5. [Pivoting telescoping arm](../robot-types/telescoping-arm.md)
6. [Differential and swerve drive](../robot-types/drivetrain.md)
7. [Climb and balance](../robot-types/climb-balance.md)

For a selected concept, finish with a subsystem/IO map, a state sequence and three injected-fault tests. Separate the hypothetical teaching design from the source team's reported implementation.
