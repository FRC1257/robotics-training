    What this experiment represents

    This is an original, simplified behavioral model. It does not execute the team's Java, reproduce contact physics, validate a robot or provide hardware tuning values. Sensor toggles are independent scenario inputs; they are not generated from the drawn piece position.

    For homed axes, Record home switch assumes a home event with the request released; it does not move to a physical switch. Disabling freezes the ideal mechanism and instantly stops its flywheel in this model; physical inertia and coast-down are omitted. The climber model does not simulate charge-station balance or prove engagement and load retention.
